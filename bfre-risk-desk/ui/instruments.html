The Balance

PLATE 2 · LEVEL 2

A new month, and you are the desk downstream. You do not get returns — you get the exposure file and the residual file, which is what BFRE's specific-risk model actually consumes. Exactly one residual has been overwritten. Two things in this file are supposed to be zero. Make them zero.

Do not hunt by trial and error — that earns nothing, and in a real file of 4,000 stocks it is not available to you. Deduce it. One cell was moved by an unknown amount δ. Check 1 hands you δ directly. Check 2 divided by Check 1 hands you the exposure of the stock it happened to — and only one stock has that exposure. Then use the steppers once.
